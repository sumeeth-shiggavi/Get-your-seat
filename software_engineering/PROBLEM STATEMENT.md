**PROBLEM STATEMENT** 



Students appearing for competitive examinations such as NEET, KCET, and JEE often face difficulties after receiving their examination results. They need to identify suitable colleges and courses based on their rank, category, preferences, budget, and eligibility.



Currently, students may have to depend on multiple sources such as counselling websites, college websites, social media, educational websites, and individual counsellors. This makes the counselling process confusing, time-consuming, and difficult to manage.



Students may also struggle with:

* Understanding their counselling options
* Finding colleges matching their rank
* Comparing colleges and courses
* Understanding previous-year cutoffs
* Preparing their choice/preference list
* Knowing important counselling dates
* Finding reliable counsellors
* Booking counselling sessions
* Managing counselling-related documents.
* 

Therefore, there is a need for a single integrated online platform that provides students with college discovery, rank-based guidance, counselling assistance, college comparison, counsellor booking, and counselling-related information in one place.
